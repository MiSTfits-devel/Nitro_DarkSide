orientation

Six things that reframe the whole core

If you've been writing an ARM-focused backport, these are the decisions that shape the core — everything below is the picture that follows from them.

01 · THE ARM7

The ARM7 core is settled RTL. All of the NDS work on this side sits in its memory map.

02 · THE ARM9

ARM946E-S grows out of the same pipeline as the ARM7: plus v5TE ops, CP15, and a cache hierarchy.

03 · WHERE THE TCMs LIVE

Not in the CPU. They sit in the bus decoder, overlaid on the map and driven by CP15 output ports.

04 · WHY CACHES AT ALL

Not an optimisation. 4 MB of main RAM lives in SDRAM; the caches are what make that survivable.

05 · PACING

Most of the console advances on clk1x. The ARM9 now uses its real 67 MHz domain at 2:1, and timing is still open.

06 · WHAT COUNTS AS DONE

A CPU-only differential ran for ten million instructions with no divergence in the generated workload.
